SECTION 01 • ABOUT US

Building a stronger future for medical education, clinical practice, and genomic research

GeneAcademy™ and the SudaGene Research Centre (SGRC) were established as an integrated medical and academic ecosystem. Together, they connect clinical education, practical simulation, professional examination preparation, genomic research, digital credential verification, and institutional support.

Our purpose is to help healthcare professionals and learners move from knowledge to confident practice while giving universities, hospitals, and research teams access to structured academic and clinical solutions.

We believe that modern healthcare education must be practical, evidence-informed, measurable, and connected to real professional opportunities. That belief guides every program, research initiative, credential, and partnership we develop.

Our Story

GeneAcademy™ was created to address a clear need: medical learners and healthcare professionals require more than theoretical instruction. They need opportunities to practise clinical skills, prepare systematically for professional examinations, document their achievements, and continue developing throughout their careers.

SGRC extends this mission into molecular medicine, genetics, oncology, bioinformatics, and research training. The two organizations work together to create a connected pathway from learning and simulation to research, professional identity, and institutional impact.

From clinical skills workshops and licensing-exam preparation to genomic analysis and academic program support, our work is designed to make professional development more organized, verifiable, and relevant to the realities of modern healthcare.

OPERATIONAL SCOPE

Our Five Areas of Work

AREA 01

Clinical training and simulation

We organize practical clinical workshops that help participants develop essential skills in a supervised learning environment. Our training portfolio includes resuscitation education, basic surgical skills, clinical simulation, and practical preparation for healthcare settings.

Where applicable, programs are delivered with qualified instructors and partner organizations. Course accreditation, licensing, and delivery arrangements are stated clearly for each individual program.

AREA 02

Medical licensing and fellowship preparation

Our Medical Training and Cases platform, MTC™, supports structured preparation for professional examinations and international pathways. It uses clinical cases and a mechanism-to-clinic learning approach to help learners connect scientific reasoning with practical decision-making.

The preparation areas may include the Sudan Medical Council pathway, MRCS-related preparation, USMLE-related study support, OET preparation, and other professional examinations. Program eligibility, curriculum scope, and examination relationships are specified on the relevant program page.

AREA 03

Digital professional identity and verification

SudaPass™ is our digital credential and professional identity concept. It is designed to support the documentation of training, professional achievements, continuing development, and relevant credentials through a structured digital record.

The system is intended to make verification clearer for professionals, institutions, and authorized third parties. Each credential and verification process should be assessed according to the specific evidence and issuing authority associated with it.

AREA 04

University and hospital solutions

We support universities, hospitals, laboratories, and healthcare organizations with academic and operational solutions. These may include curriculum recovery, examination organization, training coordination, digital systems, diagnostic equipment support, and professional development programs.

Our institutional work begins with understanding the organization’s needs and then designing a practical, measurable response that fits its academic, clinical, and operational environment.

AREA 05

Molecular medicine and genomic research

Through SGRC, we support learning and research in molecular medicine, genetics, oncology, genomics, bioinformatics, and related disciplines.

Our research-oriented activities may include molecular medicine education, research-group development, scientific writing, genomic data analysis, research supervision, and preparation for publication in peer-reviewed journals. All research outputs, affiliations, and publication claims are subject to appropriate academic and ethical review.
