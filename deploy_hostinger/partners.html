Personal profile or business profile

Every account — individual or organizational — is part of the same points system. What differs is how each one is set up and what it activates.

Personal profile

For individual academic staff

A faculty member joins under their own GA-ID, earning and using points as an individual member of the network.

Business profile

For universities, companies, and organizations

The institution sets up its own organizational profile, separate from any individual staff account. Creating this profile is what activates the points system at the institutional level.

Both feed the same ledgerbut only a business profile unlocks institutional-tier benefits

What activation unlocks for a partner institution

Once a university or organization's business profile is live, its points can go toward:

  • GLOMEt equipment & training gear

    Practical training and simulation equipment supplied through our GLOMEt partnership.

  • Institutional standing & visibility

    Recognized presence on the platform as a verified partner institution.

  • Research equipment supply

    Access to equipment support for departmental or research use.

  • Continuity contracts through Silicon Labs
