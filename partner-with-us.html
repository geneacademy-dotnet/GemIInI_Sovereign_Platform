Individual profile or business profile

Every account — individual or organisational — participates in the same GP points system. What differs is how each one is set up and what it activates.

Individual profile

For academic and clinical staff

A faculty member, clinician, or researcher joins under their own GA-ID, earning and using GP points as an individual member of the network.

Business profile

For universities, hospitals, and organisations

The institution sets up its own organisational profile, separate from any individual staff accounts. This profile activates the GP points system at the institutional level.

Both feed the same ledger only a business profile unlocks institutional-tier benefits

What activation unlocks for a partner institution

Once a university or organisation's business profile is active, its GP points can be directed toward:

  • GLOMEt equipment and training gear

    Practical simulation and training equipment supplied through our GLOMEt partnership for lab and clinical use.

  • Institutional standing and verified visibility

    Recognised presence on the platform as a verified partner institution, visible to the broader network.

  • Research and laboratory equipment access

    Access to equipment support for departmental research and applied science needs.

  • Academic continuity support

    Structured cohort management, batch tracking, and faculty coordination for institutions managing distributed learner groups.
