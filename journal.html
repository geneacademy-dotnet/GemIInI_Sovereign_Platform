Surgical Intervention Protocols in Resource-Limited Settings: A Framework for BSS-2 Graduates

Alaa Mursi Farah, FMRCS; Mohamed Gibbril, MBBS

This vignette outlines the standardized operating procedures developed for the Cairo Surgical Cohort. By bridging the Mechanism-to-Clinic (MTC) gap, the framework provides actionable surgical decision trees optimized for acute trauma management in low-resource environments, directly applying the BSS-2 curriculum.

Evidence Synthesis

Molecular Mapping of Hemoglobinopathies: Demographic Telemetry from the GemIInI Network

Safaa Abdelgadir, MSc; Isam Mohamed Elkhidir, FRCPath

An exhaustive synthesis of demographic data retrieved through the glomet-telemetry engine across 63 canonical Sudanese faculties. The paper highlights statistical clusters of hemoglobinopathies and proposes a tailored pharmacological curriculum for PGY-1 residents based on regional incidence rates.
