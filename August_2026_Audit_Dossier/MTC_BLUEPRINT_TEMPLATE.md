# Stage 1 Assessment Rubrics & Clinical Checklists: Medical Writing & Critical Appraisal Track

## 🎯 Stage 1 Overview: Logical Reconstruction (The MTC™ Cognitive Cascade)
The objective of Stage 1 is to evaluate a trainee's ability to deconstruct published clinical trials, genomic papers, and epidemiological studies into the Mechanism-to-Clinic (MTC™) 3-step cognitive cascade. Trainees must transform primary scientific data into verifiable diagnostic reasoning frameworks rather than passive literature summaries.

### THE 3-STEP MTC™ COGNITIVE CASCADE
1. **SUBCELLULAR / GENOME**: Molecular lesion / biomarker alteration; Pharmacogenomic locus
2. **PATHOPHYSIOLOGY**: Cascade of organ-system dysfunction; Compensatory failure
3. **BEDSIDE CLINICAL**: Diagnostic triage, acute management, or targeted therapy

## 📝 2. Clinical Deconstruction Checklist for Trainees
*Trainees must verify all 10 items before submitting each paper deconstruction to the Academic Office:*

- [ ] **1. PRIMARY SOURCE VERIFICATION**: Is the paper indexed in PubMed/Scopus, published within the last 5 years (or a landmark trial), with a valid DOI?
- [ ] **2. CORE BIOLOGICAL DRIVER**: Have you isolated the exact cellular/genomic/molecular mechanism (e.g., KRAS G12C mutation, SGLT2 inhibition, IL-6 cascade)?
- [ ] **3. CAUSAL CONTINUUM**: Does your pathophysiology explanation map step-by-step from the molecular mechanism to organ-level dysfunction?
- [ ] **4. BEDSIDE APPLICABILITY**: Does the scenario present an actionable clinical dilemma (e.g., initial resuscitation, diagnostic workup, pharmacotherapy selection)?
- [ ] **5. VIGNETTE REALISM**: Are patient demographics, vitals, physical exam findings, and lab values physiologically consistent?
- [ ] **6. DISTRACTOR HOMOGENEITY**: Are all 5 distractors plausible, of similar length, and clinically defensible alternatives with clear rationales for why they are incorrect?
- [ ] **7. MTC™ EXPLANATION DEPTH**: Does the explanation explicitly state: (a) Why the correct answer is right at a mechanistic level, and (b) Why each distractor fails?
- [ ] **8. STATISTICAL ACCURACY**: Are all cited percentages, sensitivity/specificity figures, and clinical endpoints exact matches to the original paper's data tables?
- [ ] **9. CODE & CANONICAL ALIGNMENT**: Is the item tagged with its respective MTC Module code (e.g., SURG-201, EM-302, RES-310) and subspecialty?
- [ ] **10. ETHICAL & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE**: Are patient scenarios free of identifying details and aligned with WHO/AHA/SMC clinical governance protocols?
